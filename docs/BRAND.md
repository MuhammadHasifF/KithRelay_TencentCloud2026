# KithRelay brand system

## Positioning

**Every care detail, carried forward.**

KithRelay turns fragmented care paperwork into a source-linked handoff that families can review together. The name combines the human idea of a trusted circle with the practical act of relaying important information without dropping its context.

## Naming screen

A preliminary public screen completed on 9 Aug 2026 found no exact KithRelay healthcare product, software company, app listing, or indexed trademark result. Live RDAP checks returned no registration record for `kithrelay.com`, `kithrelay.ai`, `kithrelay.app`, `kithrelay.org`, or `kithrelay.health` at the time checked.

This is practical hackathon due diligence, not formal legal clearance. Domain availability can change at any time, and a qualified trademark professional should perform a jurisdiction-specific search before commercial launch.

## Visual direction

- Deep navy creates a calm, serious healthcare workspace without using pure black.
- Sea-glass teal identifies primary actions and verified progress.
- Warm amber identifies deadlines and items needing attention.
- Soft off-white text reduces glare while preserving strong contrast.
- Flat surfaces, clear borders, large controls, and restrained motion keep the interface legible for older users and busy caregivers.
- The three-node route mark represents a detail moving from source document, through reconciliation, to human review.

## Voice

- Plain, calm, and factual.
- Say what the documents show, not what a person should medically do.
- Prefer “review”, “confirm”, and “source” over intelligence or automation claims.
- Never describe the product as replacing a clinician or caregiver.
