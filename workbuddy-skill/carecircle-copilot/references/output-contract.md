# Output contract

## `care_calendar.md`

Use this structure:

1. Title: `[Preferred name]'s Care Calendar`
2. Generation timestamp
3. Active event table:
   - Date
   - Time
   - Event
   - Details
   - Status
   - Source document(s)
4. Reconciliation notes:
   - superseded appointment dates;
   - medication changes;
   - uncertain or conflicting facts;
   - payment deadlines.
5. Verification notice.

Do not list an original and rescheduled appointment as two active visits. The old date belongs in the reconciliation note only.

## `briefing.md`

Use this structure:

1. Title: `Appointment Briefing — [Preferred name]`
2. Next upcoming appointment details
3. Current documented medications from the newest dated list
4. Changes and conflicts found across documents
5. Two or three neutral questions the caregiver may ask the clinician
6. Complete source-file list
7. Verification notice

Questions must request confirmation or clarification. They must not embed a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

## Required verification notice

> AI-assisted administrative summary. Verify every item against the original source documents and with a qualified healthcare professional before acting.
